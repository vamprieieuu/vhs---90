3. Dedicated Engine Architecture

Implement:

Angular Ribbon & Signal Glitch Engine

It must contain two coordinated but independently controllable subsystems.

A. Angular Sawtooth Geometry System

Responsible for the second stage’s sharp geometric distortion.

Requirements:

  • Procedural polygon generation.
  • Sawtooth and angular boundary functions.
  • Configurable angular frequency and scale.
  • Irregular polygon widths and heights.
  • Diagonal and horizontal geometric orientation.
  • Sharp threshold-based region masks.
  • Large dark polygonal regions.
  • Temporal changes in polygon placement and geometry.
  • Optional edge fragmentation.
  • Configurable geometric distortion intensity.

Use mathematically generated masks and geometric functions.

Possible approaches include piecewise linear functions, triangular/sawtooth wave functions, seeded polygon vertices, and thresholded signed-distance fields.

Do not use pre-rendered polygon images or texture overlays.

Preserve sharp boundaries. Do not blur the geometry unless a separate subtle edge treatment is explicitly enabled.

B. Spectral Chroma Ribbon System

Responsible for the colored ribbon visible during the second stage.

Requirements:

  • Procedural curved or segmented ribbon path.
  • Adjustable path direction, curvature, width, and position.
  • High-saturation spectral color distribution.
  • Green, red, blue, and purple hues.
  • Color progression along the ribbon.
  • Irregular chromatic edges.
  • Optional horizontal chroma smearing.
  • Optional RGB channel displacement.
  • Temporal variation in ribbon curvature and position.

The ribbon should remain visually structured and recognizable.

Do not replace it with a generic full-frame rainbow gradient.

The ribbon’s shape and color progression must be independently adjustable from the angular polygons.
